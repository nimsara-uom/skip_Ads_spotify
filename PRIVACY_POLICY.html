Privacy Policy for Stupefy!

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the data collection, processing, and storage practices of the "Stupefy!" browser extension (hereinafter referred to as "the Extension", "we", "us", or "our"). By installing and using the Extension, you agree to the terms outlined in this document. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use the Extension.

2. Principle of Zero Data Collection

The Extension is designed with a strict "privacy-by-design" methodology. We do not collect, harvest, transmit, distribute, sell, or monetize any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or non-personal usage data.

The Extension operates entirely locally on your device within the sandboxed environment of your web browser. There are no external servers, analytics trackers, or telemetry systems associated with the Extension.

3. Local Storage and Data Retention

To function correctly and provide a seamless user experience, the Extension utilizes your browser's local storage capabilities (specifically, the chrome.storage.local API). The data stored is strictly limited to:

  • User Preferences: Your selected operational mode (e.g., auto, mute, speed) and the overarching enabled/disabled state of the Extension.
  • Operational Statistics: A local, anonymized counter of the number of ad interruptions handled by the Extension (tracked daily and cumulatively).

This data is written to and read from your device's local hard drive exclusively. It is never transmitted over the internet. You maintain complete sovereignty over this data and may purge it at any time by uninstalling the Extension or clearing your browser's extension data.

4. Required Permissions and Justification

The Extension requires specific browser permissions to execute its core functionality. These permissions are restricted to the minimum scope necessary:

  • storage: Required solely to persist the local preferences and statistics detailed in Section 3.
  • scripting and tabs: Required to programmatically inject execution scripts into active tabs.
  • Host Permissions (*://open.spotify.com/*): The Extension restricts its operational scope exclusively to the Spotify Web Player domain. It cannot and does not access, read, or modify content on any other websites you visit.

5. Third-Party Services and Intermediaries

While the Extension interacts with the DOM (Document Object Model) of the Spotify Web Player to perform its designated functions, it is an independent, third-party tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Spotify AB. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of Spotify AB.

6. User Rights (GDPR / CCPA / CPRA)

Because the Extension does not collect or process any personal data off-device, there is no centralized database from which to request access, rectification, or deletion of personal data. All data resides physically on your machine. Your right to deletion is exercised by simply uninstalling the Extension.

7. Amendments to This Policy

We reserve the right to amend this Privacy Policy at our discretion to reflect changes in legal requirements, browser policies, or functional updates to the Extension. Any modifications will be reflected by an updated "Effective Date" at the top of this document. Continued use of the Extension following such modifications constitutes your acknowledgement and acceptance of the revised terms.

8. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, compliance, or technical concerns, please contact the developer via the official repository or support channels listed on the Chrome Web Store distribution page.
